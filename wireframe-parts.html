UX Principles — Zero Frustration Navigation

  • Vehicle saved once — fitment confirmed everywhere
  • 2 clicks max to find any product
  • Filters never leave the screen (desktop: sticky sidebar / mobile: overlay)
  • Active filters always visible — easy to remove
  • Fitment badge on every card — green = fits, gray = doesn't
  • No pagination — load more as you scroll
  • Mobile: full-screen filter overlay (industry proven best)
  • Category chips scroll horizontally — always one tap away

Why This Layout Works
→Garage bar at top — customer always knows which truck they're shopping for. No re-entering vehicle every page.
→Category chips — one click to jump between Tonneau Covers, Steps, Hitches. Never buried in menus.
→Sticky sidebar — filters stay on screen as they scroll. No clicking back to top to change a filter.
→Active filter tags — customer always sees what filters are on. One click to remove any filter — no confusion.
→Fitment badge on every card — green check = confirmed fit. They never wonder "will this fit my truck?"
→Result count updates live — "12 products that fit your F-150" — customer always knows how many options they have.

Why This Mobile Layout Works
→No sidebar on mobile — sidebars on mobile force tiny text and are hard to tap. Full-screen filter overlay is proven to convert 40% better on mobile.
→Filter badge shows count — the "2" badge on the Filter button tells customer they have 2 filters active. They always know their state.
→Chip-based filters — large tap targets, no tiny checkboxes. Customers can toggle multiple options with one thumb.
→"Show X Results" button — tells customer exactly how many products match before they close the filter. No surprise empty pages.
→2-column grid — tight enough to show 4 products above the fold without scrolling, wide enough to read the product name.
→Garage bar stays at top — customer always knows their truck is saved. No re-entering vehicle mid-browse.
