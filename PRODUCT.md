# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Truck owners in the South Bay / Signal Hill, CA area. They own F-150s, Silverados, RAMs, Tacomas. They care about their trucks — this isn't transportation, it's identity. They're researching bedliners, tonneau covers, running boards, and accessories either on a phone between jobs or on a desktop at home. They want to know they're dealing with real professionals, not a generic parts warehouse. Local loyalty matters — they want the best shop, not the cheapest click.

## Product Purpose

3J's Auto Body & Paint and RLSH Rhino Linings of Signal Hill — a dual-brand physical shop that installs truck accessories and does premium auto body / paint work. The website exists to convert truck owners into booked appointments and quote requests. Success = someone who lands on the site, finds their truck, sees the right products, and calls or fills out a quote form. The accessories catalog (tonneau covers, steps, floor liners, lighting, headache racks, towing) is backed by real fitment data from the ACES/PIES standard.

## Brand Personality

Tough, Local, Premium. Blue-collar authority with elevated craft. Not a cheap chain, not a corporate dealership — the best shop in Signal Hill that happens to carry high-end gear. Voice is direct and confident, never salesy or corporate. Earned trust through expertise, not marketing polish.

## Anti-references

- Cookie-cutter auto parts site templates (AutoZone, O'Reilly aesthetic — commodity feel, zero craft)
- Generic car dealer websites (CarGurus, Autotrader — sterile, forgettable, interchangeable)
- Purple-gradient AI slop — floating cards, overused SaaS aesthetic, fake-modern
- Big-box chain store layouts — anything that could belong to any business in any city
- Corporate homepage templates — hero with stock photo, 3-column icons, generic CTA

## Design Principles

1. **Own the block** — Every page should feel like it could only belong to 3J's. If it could be lifted and dropped onto a competitor's site, it's not good enough.
2. **Truck culture, elevated** — Respect the aesthetic of the customer (tough, industrial, capable) but execute it at a premium level. Think Raptor, not rental car.
3. **One system, no exceptions** — Fonts, colors, corner radius, spacing, button styles — identical across every single page. Nothing gets to be a special case.
4. **Show the work** — Product imagery, fitment data, real pricing, real trucks. No vague promises. Every claim is backed by something visible.
5. **Mobile earns its keep** — Most visitors are on a phone. The mobile experience is not a shrunk desktop — it's the primary design surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High contrast ratios given the dark color palette. Touch targets minimum 44px on mobile. No reliance on color alone to convey meaning.
