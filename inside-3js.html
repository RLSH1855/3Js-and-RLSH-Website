Our Story

A Family Built on Doing It Right

3J's Auto Body & Paint was founded in 1991 by Jimmy Sferrazza at our same location in Signal Hill — and we haven't moved since. The "3J's" stands for three generations of family: Grandfather Jim, his son Jimmy, and now Grandson James.

We are a family owned and operated facility. What started as a collision repair shop has grown into a full-service truck build and accessories destination — but our values have never changed. We treat every vehicle like it's our own.

Certified Installer

Certified Rhino Liner Installer

We're a certified Rhino Liner installer, offering professional spray-on protection that's built to last. Unlike drop-in liners, this is a permanent application that bonds directly to your truck bed for maximum durability.

It provides a tough, textured finish that protects against scratches, dents, rust, and everyday wear. We also offer applications for wheel wells and undercarriages for added protection where it matters most.

Most installs can be completed the same day and are backed by a lifetime warranty—so you can count on it for the long haul.

Step by Step

Your Repair Journey

What to expect every step of the way — from the moment the accident happens to the day you drive away with confidence.

First things first—make sure everyone is safe. Then contact your insurance company to start your claim. When you're ready, give us a call, stop by, or schedule your appointment online. We'll take it from there.
We begin with a detailed initial estimate based on visible damage. From there, we work directly with your insurance company to review and approve the repairs.

If you need a rental, we can help coordinate one through our onsite partner, Enterprise. We'll also set you up with updates via phone, text, or email—whichever you prefer—so you're always in the loop.
Once your vehicle is in the shop, we carefully disassemble the damaged areas to uncover any hidden damage.

If anything additional is found, we'll communicate with you and your insurance company right away to review and approve any necessary updates—no surprises.
With everything approved, repairs officially begin. We'll provide you with an estimated completion date and keep you updated throughout the process.
Your vehicle is repaired following manufacturer guidelines to ensure safety and proper restoration. After repairs are completed, we perform a detailed quality inspection to make sure everything meets our standards.

We'll also keep you informed and confirm your expected delivery date.
Our team prepares the surface through careful sanding and smoothing before applying primer, color-matched paint, and a protective clear coat.

Once complete, we perform a second quality inspection to ensure a flawless finish.
Your vehicle is reassembled with precision, ensuring all components are properly installed and functioning as they should.

You'll receive a final delivery reminder so you know exactly when your vehicle will be ready.
Before pickup, we thoroughly clean and prepare your vehicle. If required, we perform advanced driver-assistance system (ADAS) calibrations to ensure all safety features are working correctly.

Your Service Advisor completes a final inspection to ensure everything is perfect.
Your vehicle is ready on time—just as promised. You can drive away with confidence knowing your repairs were done right.
We stand behind our work with a written limited lifetime warranty. As long as you own your vehicle, you can count on us to stand behind the repairs we've performed.
