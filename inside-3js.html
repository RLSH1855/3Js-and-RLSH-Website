Our Story

A Family Built on Doing It Right

3J's Auto Body & Paint was founded in 1991 by Jimmy Sferrazza at our same location in Signal Hill — and we haven't moved since. The "3J's" stands for three generations of family: Grandfather Jim, his son Jimmy, and now Grandson James.

We are a family owned and operated facility. What started as a collision repair shop has grown into a full-service truck build and accessories destination — but our values have never changed. We treat every vehicle like it's our own.

We're not here just to fix your truck. We're here to take care of you through the whole process — from the first phone call to the day you drive out.

Our Promise

Lifetime Warranty

We stand behind our work on every vehicle with a written limited lifetime warranty. We get the job done right and get you back on the road safely. Guaranteed.

While we're confident the repairs are done right, we're also available to help if something goes wrong. We'll correct the problem free of charge as long as you own your vehicle.

Insurance · Cash · Lease

Direct Repair · Out of Pocket · Lease Turn-In

We work directly with all major insurance companies to help streamline the repair process and reduce stress after an accident. Whether your repair is insurance-covered or being paid out of pocket, our team provides clear communication, honest estimates, and guidance every step of the way.

Have a lease turn-in coming up? We can help get your vehicle restored to proper factory condition quickly and efficiently, addressing wear-and-tear, damage, and cosmetic issues so you can return it with confidence and avoid unexpected charges.

Built to Perform

State-of-the-Art Equipment

Proper repairs require the right tools, and we've invested heavily in advanced, industry-leading equipment to ensure precision and quality on every job. From frame straightening systems to computerized measuring, paint-matching technology, and modern refinishing tools, our shop is equipped to handle virtually any make and model.

We also use high-quality materials designed for durability and long-lasting results, ensuring your repairs not only look great — but hold up over time.

People First

Family Atmosphere

From the moment you walk through our doors, you're treated like family. We believe professional service should still feel personal, approachable, and respectful.

Our team keeps you informed throughout the entire repair process, explains everything clearly, and provides honest updates so you always know where things stand. We value transparency, communication, and trust — and we never recommend or perform unnecessary work.

At our shop, you're not just another repair order — you're part of our community.

Certified Installer

Certified Rhino Liner Installer

We are a certified Rhino Liner installer, offering professional-grade spray-on protection — not drop-in liners. Our application creates a permanent bond that helps protect your truck bed from impact, rust, and wear.

The finish is durable, textured, and built to last, with same-day service available in many cases. Backed by a lifetime warranty, our Rhino Liner installations are also available for wheel wells and undercarriage applications for full vehicle durability and protection.
