Headache Racks — Common Questions
What is a headache rack and why do I need one?+
A headache rack (also called a cab protector) mounts at the front of your truck bed between the cab and the cargo area. It protects your rear cab window and passengers from shifting cargo — whether you're hauling lumber, tools, or equipment. It also gives you a solid mounting point for work lights, backup cameras, and more.
What's the difference between the Open, Safety, and Louvered styles?+
Open Rack — a tubular frame with no infill panel. Great visibility, maximum airflow, and a rugged look. Best for light mounting.

Safety Rack — has a perforated steel center panel. Provides full cab window protection from small debris and cargo shift. Most popular in work truck applications.

Louvered Rack — angled horizontal louvers give it the signature BackRack look. Maximum cab protection, strong and heavy duty.
Will a headache rack work with my tonneau cover?+
Yes — BackRack makes specific tonneau cover hardware kits and headache rack adapters designed to work with most major tonneau cover brands including BAK, Extang, Retrax, and TruXedo. When you come in, our techs will confirm compatibility before installation.
Can I mount lights on a headache rack?+
Yes — BackRack frames are pre-drilled with mounting holes to accept standard round and rectangular lights. If you're adding Rigid Industries or Baja Designs lighting, we can install the whole setup at once so everything is wired cleanly and looks factory.
Do you install headache racks at the shop?+
Yes — we source, supply, and install. Bring your truck in and our techs handle everything. Get a quote and we'll confirm fitment, pricing, and schedule your install. No measuring tape required on your end.
