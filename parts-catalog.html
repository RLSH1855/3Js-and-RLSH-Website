Consider how often you need full bed access, your local weather, and your security needs. Hard folding covers offer the best balance of security and access. Roll-up covers are the easiest to use daily. Retractable covers offer the most premium look and function. Not sure? Yes. Most hard folding covers can be driven in the fully open (folded against the cab) or partially folded position. Roll-up covers can be driven rolled up. Always ensure the cover is secured before driving at highway speeds.
Tonneau covers are weather-resistant. Quality covers with EPDM rubber seals (like BAKFlip and Extang) provide excellent water management and keep the vast majority of rain out. They are not 100% watertight, so avoid submerging them. For maximum protection, look for covers with dual-action seals.
