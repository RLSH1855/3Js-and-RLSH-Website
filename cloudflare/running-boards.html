Common Questions

Cab Length steps run from the front door to the rear door of the cab — ideal for most trucks and cleaner in appearance. Wheel to Wheel steps extend all the way to the rear wheel arch, covering the full rocker panel. They're a longer step and better for trucks used for work, camping, or hauling where you climb in and out from different spots.

Running board length is built specifically for your cab configuration. A Crew Cab step is longer than an Extended Cab step, which is longer than a Standard Cab step. Using the wrong length looks off and may not mount correctly — that's why we filter by cab type to give you the exact fit.

N-Fab offers a 5-year finish warranty and a lifetime workmanship warranty on their nerf steps. Our installation is also covered by 3J's workmanship guarantee.

Standard N-Fab nerf steps mount above the rocker panel and add minimal height below the door. For lifted trucks, we recommend verifying clearance at the time of install — our techs will let you know if there are any fitment concerns before we start.
