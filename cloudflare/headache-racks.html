A headache rack is a steel rack mounted behind the cab of a pickup truck that protects your rear window from shifting cargo. It also provides a mounting platform for lights, strobe bars, and other accessories — and gives your truck a clean, professional look.

The Open Rack Frame has a classic open design with maximum visibility. The Safety Rack Frame has a perforated steel panel for full window protection. The Louvered Frame features angled louvers for extra strength and a signature look. All three mount the same way and accept the same accessories.
