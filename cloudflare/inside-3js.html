Our Story

A Family Built on Doing It Right

3J's Auto Body & Paint was founded in 1991 by Jimmy Sferrazza at our same location in Signal Hill — and we haven't moved since. The "3J's" stands for three generations of family: Grandfather Jim, his son Jimmy, and now Grandson James.

We are a family owned and operated facility. What started as a collision repair shop has grown into a full-service truck build and accessories destination — but our values have never changed. We treat every vehicle like it's our own.

We're not here just to fix your truck. We're here to take care of you through the whole process — from the first phone call to the day you drive out.

Step by Step

Your Repair Journey

What to expect every step of the way — from the moment the accident happens to the day you drive away.

Step 01

The Accident Happens

Make sure everyone is safe, then contact your insurance to start your claim. Call or stop by — we'll take it from there.

Step 02

Your Estimate & Getting Started

We write a detailed estimate and work directly with your insurance to approve repairs. Enterprise rental coordination available onsite.

Step 03

Disassembly & Inspection

We carefully disassemble damaged areas to uncover hidden damage. If anything additional is found, we communicate with you and your insurance right away — no surprises.

Step 04

Repairs Begin

With everything approved, repairs officially begin. We provide an estimated completion date and keep you updated throughout.

Step 05

Structural & Body Repairs

Repaired following manufacturer guidelines. A detailed quality inspection follows every repair stage.

Step 06

Paint & Refinishing

Surface prep, primer, computer-matched color paint, and a clear coat — with a quality check at every stage.

Step 07

Reassembly

All components precisely reinstalled and verified. You receive a final delivery reminder so you know exactly when your vehicle will be ready.

Step 08

Final Quality Assurance

We clean your vehicle, perform any required ADAS calibrations, and your Service Advisor does a final walkthrough.

Step 09

Vehicle Ready for Pickup

Your vehicle is ready on time, as promised. Drive away with confidence knowing every repair was done right.

Step 10

Lifetime Warranty

Every repair is backed by our written limited lifetime warranty — for as long as you own the vehicle.

Industry Certified

I-CAR Gold Class Certified

Your vehicle is a big investment, and you shouldn't trust it with just anyone. Our shop holds the I-CAR Gold Class designation—the highest level of training in the collision repair industry.

That means our technicians are continuously trained in the latest repair techniques, materials, and vehicle technologies. Whether it's a minor repair or significant structural damage, we have the knowledge and experience to restore your vehicle the right way.

For you, that means peace of mind knowing your vehicle is being repaired by professionals who take pride in their work and prioritize your safety.

Built to Perform

State-of-the-Art Equipment

Your vehicle deserves more than a quick fix—it deserves to be repaired properly. That's why we've invested in advanced, state-of-the-art equipment to ensure every repair is done with precision.

From structural repairs to paint matching, we use high-quality tools and materials designed to deliver long-lasting results. This allows us to work on all makes and models while maintaining the level of quality your vehicle deserves.

Fast & Affordable

Paintless Dent Repair

Got small dents, door dings, or hail damage? We offer paintless dent repair as a fast and cost-effective way to restore your vehicle without repainting.

Because we preserve your original factory finish, the process is typically quicker and more affordable than traditional repairs. It's a great option for keeping your vehicle looking its best without unnecessary work.

Safety First

Glass Repair & Replacement

Damaged or cracked glass can affect both your safety and visibility. We provide professional glass repair and replacement services to get your vehicle back to proper condition.

Whether it's a windshield, side window, or other glass, we make sure everything is installed correctly and meets safety standards—so you can drive with confidence.

Certified Installer

Certified Rhino Liner Installer

We're a certified Rhino Liner installer, offering professional spray-on protection that's built to last. Unlike drop-in liners, this is a permanent application that bonds directly to your truck bed for maximum durability.

It provides a tough, textured finish that protects against scratches, dents, rust, and everyday wear. We also offer applications for wheel wells and undercarriages for added protection where it matters most.

Most installs can be completed the same day and are backed by a lifetime warranty—so you can count on it for the long haul.

People First

Family Atmosphere

We believe great service starts with how you're treated. From the moment you walk through our doors, you'll notice the difference—we treat our customers like family.

That means being honest, respectful, and transparent in everything we do. We take the time to explain the process, answer your questions, and keep you updated every step of the way so you always feel comfortable and informed.

It's all part of what we mean when we say we're "meeting great people by accident." Even in an unexpected situation, our goal is to turn your experience into a positive one by taking care of you the right way. No confusing lingo. No runaround. Just real people who genuinely care about doing right by you.
