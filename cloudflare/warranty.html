What's Covered

Lifetime Protection
Against Defects

Your Rhino Liner is backed by a Nationwide Limited Lifetime Bedliner Warranty covering every truck bed liner applied by a certified North American applicator — including 3J's Auto Body right here in Signal Hill, CA.

Coverage protects against three specific manufacturing and application defects for as long as you own your truck.

Cracking

Surface or structural cracks caused by application defects

Bubbling

Air pockets or bubbles forming beneath the liner surface

Peeling

Delamination or separation of the liner from the truck bed

Eligibility

Who Qualifies

The warranty applies to the following conditions. All three must be met for coverage to be valid.

  • Original equipment truck bed with factory paint intact at time of application
  • Vehicle used for personal, non-commercial purposes only
  • Original owner only — warranty does not transfer upon vehicle resale
  • Application performed by a certified Rhino Linings applicator after October 2006
Making a Claim

How to Submit
a Warranty Claim

If you believe your bedliner has a covered defect, here's what you'll need to start the process:

  • Original invoice from your Rhino Linings installation appointment
  • Photographs clearly documenting the damaged or defective areas
  • Submit your claim online through the Rhino Linings warranty portal

If you've moved since your installation, Rhino Linings' nationwide network of certified applicators can assist with your claim locally.

Limitations & Exclusions

What's Not Covered

The following are explicitly excluded from warranty coverage:

  • Applications performed before October 2006
  • Rhino Linings applied to areas other than the truck bed (e.g., exterior body panels, floors)
  • Damage caused by misuse, accidents, or commercial vehicle use
  • Vehicles where the warranty has been transferred to a subsequent owner
