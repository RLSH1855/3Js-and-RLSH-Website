Our Process

How We Install It.

Every hitch job at 3J's goes through the same three steps — no shortcuts, no guessing. We spec the right hardware for your vehicle and tow rating, install it to torque spec, and test it before you leave the lot.

Step 01

We Spec It

We confirm your vehicle's tow rating, hitch class, and exactly what you're towing — then match the right hitch, ball mount, and wiring for the job.

Step 02

We Install It

Hardware goes on using factory mounting points on most applications. Wiring is routed clean and zip-tied — no exposed wires, no shortcuts.

Step 03

We Test It

Every bolt is torqued to spec and every trailer light and brake connection is tested before you tow away. Done right, the first time.
