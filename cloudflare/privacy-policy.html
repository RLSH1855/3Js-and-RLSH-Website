Protecting Yourself from Phishing & Identity Theft

As part of our dedication to internet safety, keep these tips in mind whether you're shopping on our site or anywhere else:

  • Pay attention to the email sender — a return address from an unrelated source, or a well-known company name sent from a webmail account, is an obvious red flag.
  • Phishing emails often use well-reputed company names as a cover. If in doubt, forward the message to the real company's customer service and have them confirm it's genuine.
  • Be wary of messages that create a false sense of urgency by threatening account suspension or demanding you "confirm" account information.
  • Watch for obvious spelling and grammatical errors, or overuse of capitalization and exclamation points.
  • Most companies will not ask for personal information — social security, credit card, tax ID, or PIN numbers — in an email message.
  • Beware of attachments or links in email messages. For embedded links, verify the web address clearly states the company being mentioned, and never visit a raw IP address (a series of numbers separated by periods).
  • For e-commerce, shop only with trusted sellers certified with data encryption (SSL). Look for a web address that begins with "https" and the padlock icon in your browser's address bar.
