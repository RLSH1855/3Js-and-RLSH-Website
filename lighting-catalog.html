Choose Your Beam Pattern

Not all off-road lights are the same. The beam pattern determines where and how far your light reaches. Pick the right one for how you drive.

Narrow · ~10°
Spot

Maximum distance, minimal spread. Pinpoints hazards far ahead. Best for high-speed trails and open desert.

Ultra Narrow · ~8°
Hyperspot

Pinpoint distance — perfect for high-speed desert runs where you need to see furthest down the trail.

Moderate · ~20°
Driving

360-Series dependable forward-facing lighting. Great balance of distance and spread for general trail driving.

Medium · ~45°
Flood

Wide spread near the vehicle. Great for tight trails, rock crawling and seeing obstacles to the sides.

Mixed Spread
Combo

Blends distance and spread in one light. Most versatile option for mixed terrain and all-around use.

Very Wide · ~115°+
Scene

Floods a wide area with light. Perfect for campsites, work areas, or illuminating the trail around your rig when stationary.

Very Wide · ~120°+
Diffused

Frosted lens scatters light evenly — no harsh shadows or glare. Ideal for backup lights and close-range work areas.

Not sure which to pick? Tell us how you use your truck and we'll recommend the right beam for your build.
