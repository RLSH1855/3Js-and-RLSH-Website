Surface Technology

FRP.
The Hard Shell.

The F1's Fiberglass Reinforced Polymer top skin is a composite material that resists the dents, dings, and scratches that standard aluminum can't shrug off. It also runs cooler to the touch in direct sun.

Beneath the FRP skin, heavy-duty aluminum panels give the cover its structural strength — the same tested metal construction that built BAKFlip's reputation.

  • FRP composite top panel
  • Heavy-duty aluminum underframe
  • Premium density foam core
  • Advanced impact-resistant construction
Full Bed Access

Flip It Up.
Drive With It Open.

The F1's design lets you drive with the cover in any position — closed, folded, or flipped up flat against the cab. The heavy-duty hardware and upgraded latch housing keep it locked securely in every mode.

  • Drive open, folded, or closed
  • Automatic latching — every panel
  • 3rd brake light visibility (short/standard beds)
  • Locks with tailgate
